- **Evidence (measured in-engine, `mats` probe):** every material of `props/q_trees/*` (Tree_1 ×784, Pine_3 ×270, Pine_1 ×76, Tree_8 ×58, DeadTree_1 ×435, DeadTree_5 ×33 ≈ **1,650 instances**) and every `props/vostok_extra/MS_*` prop (crate ×20, candle ×9, plank pile ×10, sawbuck ×10, board ×7, trash ×7, composter ×6, pallet ×5, cable reel ×5, fireplace tower ×3, cabinet ×3, carpet ×2, dartboard, pole lights in `rows`) renders with `albedo (0.906,0.906,0.906), tex:false`. The GLBs ship `images: 0`, baseColorFactor 0.8.
- **Root cause — ENGINE BUG (surfaces.gd:155–204):** `GSurf.fix_untextured_props` is wired in (area_builder.gd:421/468) but `_is_stripped()` compares `albedo_color` to `GLTF_DEFAULT_ALBEDO := 0.8 ± 0.02`, while Godot's glTF importer stores baseColorFactor 0.8 as **sRGB 0.906** → the fingerprint never matches → the repair is a no-op for every untextured kit. Fix in the template: compare against `Color(0.8,0.8,0.8).linear_to_srgb()` (≈0.906) as well as 0.8. Do NOT patch surfaces.gd locally (sync-engine overwrites it) — put this under an **ENGINE BUG** heading in the PR body.
- **Data-side fix for THIS build (do this regardless):** replace the q_trees kit (Tree_1/Tree_8/Pine_1/Pine_3/DeadTree_*) with textured trees (the Meshy `hawthorn_windswept` reads well — `G08`, `G12`) and swap/drop the MS_* dressing (crates, candles, fireplaces, carpets, dartboard, cabinet, pallets, plank piles, sawbucks, pole lights) for textured Meshy/library props. The good news: Meshy furniture, Mason buildings, Rock_Medium, Plant_7, gorse/heather/bracken cards, hero/NPC/enemy models all render textured.
