# Kestrel Bay

A grey, rain-soaked fishing town on steep coastal cliffs — an open world built as **data** on the
Gogi `godot-tmpl-rpg` engine (Godot 4.7.1, Compatibility renderer, `nothreads` web export, also
native-playable via `world.json`).

**Play:** https://preview.myapping.com/cloud-cbnqgdi78kdlrr3elmbb/

## The place

- A +26 m cliff-top plateau ringed by a real sea cliff (island falloff, ~45°) over grey-green water.
- The **harbour** sits in a cove at +1.5 m; a **switchback road** (a staircase of ns/ew legs on the
  45°-oriented cove head) climbs to the town. Quay dressed with creels, nets, fish boxes, bollards,
  benches, a jetty, a moored fishing boat and a battered blue truck.
- The **town** straddles the cove on the cliff top: High Street, the Kestrel Arms pub, houses and
  terraces, a chandlery, sheds; the **chapel** on the hill behind; the **lighthouse** out on
  Kestrel Point with stairs all the way up.
- Down the coast a second narrow inlet holds the **wreck of the trawler Marguerite** and the
  **sea caves** where the smugglers are holed up (melee rushers + rifle stand-offs).

## Enterable (Mason-compiled, walk-gate verified)

lighthouse · pub (rooms upstairs) · harbour office · net shed · chapel · one house · the wreck ·
the sea caves. Every other house and shed is shut.

## People

Meshy-generated characters: the player, harbour master Alwyn, Morwenna behind the bar, Dai on his
stool, Old Hal and Jory on the quay benches, Pasco mending nets, the lighthouse keeper, the verger,
wandering villagers, and the smugglers. Talk with USE — replies are generated by the shared NPC
brain and spoken aloud.

## Engine note (one intentional patch)

`interaction.gd` carries a one-line fix over the `godot-tmpl-rpg` template: `add_npc` grounded each talk-NPC
with a WORLD-space AABB, so on any terrain world every NPC landed at world y≈0 (buried under the quay/town).
The fix subtracts the model's own world height first. `verify.mjs` reports this file as engine drift by design;
re-syncing the engine (`sync-engine.mjs`) will revert it until the template adopts the fix.

## Behaviour is data

`world.json` carries terrain, water, sky cycle, cells, vehicles, weapons, regions, `rules`/`vars`/
`hud` and the `director` (title screen, quest chain, boss bar, victory). `quests.json` holds the
two-quest story. `structures.json` holds the Mason building specs. No game-authored scripts.

## Build

```bash
/workspace/godot --headless --path . --import
/workspace/godot --headless --path . --export-release "Web" out/index.html
cp world.json quests.json out/ && cp -R models audio out/
```
